Preparation Meetings

For the day to run smoothly, it is essential that all attendees take part in both preparation meetings. They let teams meet in advance, agree their problem statement and get the technical workspace working before arriving in Newcastle.

Meeting Date and time Purpose
1. Team meeting Monday 30 November · time TBC per team Meet your team and receive your problem statement
2. EODH workspace introduction Wednesday 2 December · 11:00–12:00 Introduction to the EODH technical workspace

1. Team meeting — Monday 30 November

Time: to be confirmed for each group
Format: online

In this session you will:

  • Meet the other members of your team
  • Receive your team’s problem statement from our policy partners
  • Discuss what decision the problem statement supports and who would use the output
  • Agree roles for the day (e.g. data lead, policy lead, presenter)
Note

Can’t attend?
If you can’t make your team’s slot, you can contribute by email instead. Let the organisers know in advance and we’ll share the problem statement and ask for your input on scope and roles.

2. EODH workspace introduction — Wednesday 2 December, 11:00–12:00

Format: online

The Earth Observation Data Hub team will introduce the technical workspace you’ll use on the day. The session covers:

  • Creating your EODH account and signing in to the Hub platform
  • Joining your team’s workspace
  • Finding datasets in the resource catalogue
  • Running a starter notebook against the hackathon data
  • Q&A
Tip

Before 2 December
Create your EODH account (with a GitHub, Google or Microsoft login) and send us the email address you used, following Steps 1–2 of Getting Started with EODH.

Policy participants are very welcome at this session too — seeing what the workspace can do will help when shaping your team’s question — but it is most important for technical participants.
